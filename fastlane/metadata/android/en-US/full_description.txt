Echo Tiles lets you load your own audio files and generates responsive rhythm levels tailored to your songs.

Whether you love EDM, classical piano, pop hits, or indie beats, tap along to the music you already own—completely offline, private, and ad-free.

How to Play:
1. Select any audio track stored on your device.
2. Tap the rhythm tiles as they cascade down the lanes.
3. Keep your streak alive to build combos and set new high scores!
